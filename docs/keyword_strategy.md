# Keyword and competitor context

Last checked: 2026-08-30

## Why this site exists

The local `findnewword` tool combines Google suggestion harvesting with Google Trends comparison over the latest seven days and Worldwide geography. The working model is:

1. Find a newly rising product or game term before the results page fills up.
2. Confirm that interest is accelerating rather than relying on a static keyword-volume estimate.
3. Publish a focused site while the information supply is still thin.
4. Earn search traffic by answering the first questions real users ask.
5. Add advertising only after the site has useful sessions and compliant privacy controls.

Garden of Witches entered version 1.0 on 2026-08-28. The exact-match competitor `gardenofwitches.wiki` was already live when this project was reviewed. Our canonical domain is `www.gardenofwitches.shop`.

## Competitive gap

The competitor gives first-time players a visible Beginner Guide CTA, a first-run table and concrete advice for controls, room rewards and defeat. The original `.shop` build had strong visual design and deeper build material, but its first screen sent a new player to best-build pages before explaining the basic loop.

The 2026-08-30 revision changes the contest from “more wiki words” to “faster beginner problem resolution”:

- Homepage primary CTA now starts the first run.
- The first screen routes controls, map choices and missing progress separately.
- The Beginner Guide now follows the player from Sil's House through the first room, door choice, major fight and post-run review.
- `rooms-and-map.html` answers a long-tail question with a developer-sourced explanation of authored rooms and randomized placement/rewards.
- `upgrades-reset.html` distinguishes normal run resets from a current permanent-progression bug and tells players how to protect the save.

## Evidence used for topic selection

Current Steam discussions showed live questions about controls, WASD behavior, perfect dash/guard, upgrade resets, synergy changes, missing weapons/spells and procedural generation. Only questions with enough reliable evidence became pages. The site does not publish a first-boss or perfect-dash answer until current 1.0 evidence supports one.

## Next content queue

Prioritize topics only when search or community evidence and factual support both exist:

1. First boss survival guide, after a current 1.0 capture or official answer exists.
2. Exact weapon and spell unlock guide, after reliable unlock states can be reproduced.
3. Upgrade-reset status update when Team Tapas ships or documents a fix.
4. Chapter-specific beginner pages when real player questions reveal repeatable blockers.

Do not create thin pages merely to expand the sitemap. One page should own one search intent, answer it above the fold and link to the next player task.
