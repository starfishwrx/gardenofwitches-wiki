Garden of Witches Broken Scissors Build: Sharpness

Stay in range, gain Sharpness from hits and use Spells to keep the Sharpness engine moving.

Quick answer

Broken Scissors + Rush + Cat’s Eye Ring creates a clear loop: hit enemies to gain Sharpness, use Rush to reconnect or pull threaded targets, and use Spells to add Sharpness through Cat’s Eye Ring. At 100% Sharpness, Broken Scissors attacks become critical hits.

Documented loadout pieces

Hunger

Preview role: expand the Sharpness ceiling, but lose it if you stop gaining Sharpness.

Frenzy

Preview role: turn current Sharpness into attack speed.

Cat’s Eye Ring

Preview role: gain Sharpness when a Spell is used.

How to play the loop

  1. Enter with a target cluster. Broken Scissors deals damage within range, so isolated whiffs do not build the resource.
  2. Use Rush to restore contact. The 1.0 version creates needles and threads; a second use can pull connected enemies toward Sil.
  3. Spend a Spell before Sharpness stalls. Cat’s Eye Ring links Spell use back into the weapon resource.
  4. Hold the crit window. Once Sharpness reaches 100%, keep landing attacks rather than resetting the loop.

Two practical branches

Uptime branch: Hunger

Take this direction when the room layout, movement skill and target behavior allow frequent hits. Enter a cluster with Rush available, build Sharpness without long empty swings and avoid spending the crit window while enemies are scattering. If the previewed loss condition repeatedly triggers, the theoretical ceiling is not helping the actual run.

Conversion branch: Frenzy

Use this direction when the build reaches Sharpness reliably and benefits more from faster attack output. Faster actions do not remove the need to choose safe contact. Treat the branch as a way to capitalize on the resource, not permission to stand inside every attack.

Fallback when the named reward is missing

Keep three functions: approach, uptime and survival. A movement option can replace some of Rush's positioning value; a fast Spell can preserve a Spell-use trigger; a control or recovery reward can protect the time needed to rebuild Sharpness. Avoid taking a bonus that only activates at range if the weapon still requires close contact.

  1. Confirm the weapon can land repeated hits in the current room.
  2. Save movement for the moment contact breaks.
  3. Use the Spell only when it also solves positioning, control or a confirmed Relic trigger.
  4. At full Sharpness, attack a stable target instead of chasing a nearly defeated one.
  5. Reset before greed turns the crit window into lost health.

Managing the Sharpness window

Think in three phases: build, convert and exit. During the build phase, land reliable attacks rather than swinging at empty space. During conversion, spend the full-gauge crit state on a cluster or durable target. During exit, leave before enemy pressure turns the window into a trade. The next approach begins only when movement and target position support another cycle.

Rush belongs at the transition between phases. Using it too early can leave no approach tool when contact breaks. Using it only as panic movement can waste its offensive setup. The useful timing is the point where its confirmed movement and thread behavior restores a real attack opportunity.

Practice without ideal drops

Run the base Broken Scissors loop before judging Hunger, Frenzy or Cat's Eye Ring. Approach, land enough real hits to observe Sharpness, leave safely and repeat. Once that motion works, add one interaction and watch whether it improves uptime or merely adds complexity.

During a failed run, classify the failure. If enemies escape, improve approach or control. If Sil takes damage while staying close, shorten the conversion window or add recovery. If Sharpness builds but the crit state finds no target, change target selection. Only a confirmed tooltip mismatch calls for changing the written mechanic.

For a reproducible report, capture the complete build-build-exit cycle rather than a single full-gauge frame. Include the current tooltips and compare one branch on the same encounter. This separates player execution from reward luck and makes the next correction concrete.

When to choose this build

Choose Broken Scissors when the player can read close-range openings, reconnect after movement and spend a full Sharpness state on a target that remains in reach. Avoid presenting it as best when the current challenge repeatedly forces long disengagement or the player cannot see incoming attacks while maintaining contact.

The route remains valid without every previewed reward because its stable question is functional: can the loadout build, preserve and convert Sharpness safely? Use Hunger, Frenzy and Cat's Eye Ring only after the current tooltip confirms that they solve one of those jobs.

Frequently asked questions

How does Broken Scissors work?

Broken Scissors gains Sharpness when its attacks hit. The official preview states that all attacks become critical hits when Sharpness reaches 100%.

Which Imprint should I take for Broken Scissors?

Hunger supports a larger but more fragile Sharpness pool, while Frenzy converts Sharpness into attack speed. Verify the live 1.0 wording before relying on preview values.

What Relic supports Broken Scissors?

Cat’s Eye Ring was officially previewed as a way to gain Sharpness whenever a Spell is used.
