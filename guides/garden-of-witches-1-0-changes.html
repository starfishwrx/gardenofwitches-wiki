Garden of Witches 1.0 Update: Combat & Progression

The combat system, progression, controls and endgame all changed. This is the boundary older guides miss.

Direct answer

Version 1.0 removed the old Attribute, Synergy and Rune systems, rebuilt skills around Scissors, Spells, Imprints and Relics, added charging/combo/channeling mechanics, made Keyboard & Mouse the default, added Epilogues 2 and 3, added real-time DPS, and moved high-difficulty play into a post-Epilogue 3 Challenge Mode.

Combat and build changes

  • Removed: Attribute, Synergy and Rune systems.
  • Added to skills: Charging, Combos and Channeling.
  • Reworked: Sharpness, Thread, Flower and Crow became identities of particular skills.
  • Expanded: Level 3 progression can lead to one of three Imprints.
  • Repositioned: Relics now connect skills and create cross-skill interactions.

Story and progression

The game contains five main chapters plus Epilogues 1, 2 and 3. New Scissors and Spells unlock through progress and other conditions. Challenge Mode unlocks after Epilogue 3.

Controls and measurement

Keyboard & Mouse is the default in 1.0 and supports free aiming and mouse-button actions. Keyboard-Only remains available. A new real-time DPS UI lets players compare build output.

Quick answer

If a guide tells you to stack Attribute, Synergy or Rune systems, it targets an obsolete build. Version 1.0 moves decisions into skill-specific inputs, Level 3 Imprints, Relic connections, Armory loadouts, expanded Epilogues and post-Epilogue 3 Challenge Mode.

Returning-player checklist

  1. Open settings and select the intended control scheme.
  2. Visit the Armory and rebuild the loadout with current Scissors, Spells and Special Skill.
  3. Read every current tooltip instead of translating an old Rune recipe.
  4. Push one skill toward its Imprint branch and connect skills through a relevant Relic.
  5. Use real-time DPS for a same-encounter comparison.
  6. Complete Epilogues 1–3 before looking for Challenge Mode.

Use the new DPS display without fooling yourself

Keep chapter, encounter and core loadout fixed. Change one Imprint or Relic, then compare sustained output, clear time and damage taken. A higher peak can still be worse when the new input causes more interruption or setup time. Version 1.0 gives the measurement tool; the player still needs a controlled comparison.

Frequently asked questions

What systems were removed in Garden of Witches 1.0?

The official launch notes state that the old Attribute, Synergy and Rune systems were removed and their roles were redistributed across Scissors, Spells, Imprints and Relics.

Does Garden of Witches support mouse controls?

Yes. Keyboard and Mouse is the default in version 1.0, with free mouse aiming and mouse-button assignments. Keyboard-Only and Gamepad remain available.

When does Challenge Mode unlock?

Challenge Mode unlocks after clearing Epilogue 3.
