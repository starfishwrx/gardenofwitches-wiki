Garden of Witches Challenge Mode Guide: Unlock & Enter

Use the Epilogue 3-cleared save, prepare inside the Challenge Mode version of Sil's House, then leave through the door.

Direct answer

Challenge Mode unlocks after you clear Epilogue 3. Keep and continue that cleared save. The game loads the Challenge Mode version of Sil's House; set the available challenge options, prepare the Armory loadout, and leave through the door to begin.

Quick answer

  1. Clear Chapters 1–5 and Epilogues 1–3.
  2. Return to the title flow without replacing the cleared save.
  3. Select Continue on the Epilogue 3-cleared save.
  4. Confirm Sil's House shows the Challenge Mode state.
  5. Prepare the loadout and exit through the door.

Prepare before leaving Sil's House

Challenge Mode is the point where a coherent loadout matters more than an attractive list of bonuses. Equip Scissors whose input rhythm you can sustain under pressure, a Spell that covers its weak moment and a Special Skill with one clear job. Use the real-time DPS UI for comparisons, but read damage taken and clear consistency alongside the number.

  • Broken Scissors route: confirm you can maintain contact long enough to build Sharpness.
  • Magic Scissors route: confirm there is enough space to cross the first charge gauge and aim.
  • Keep one fallback Imprint branch for runs where the preferred reward does not appear.
  • Read the current 1.0 tooltip for previewed Relics before depending on detailed values.

First Challenge Mode test

Begin with the stable story-clear loadout rather than changing every Armory slot. Select a manageable challenge setup, run a repeatable combat window and observe where the build breaks. Use the real-time DPS display, clear time and damage taken together. Then change one component or one difficulty choice.

For Broken Scissors, measure how often the mode forces contact breaks before full Sharpness. For Magic Scissors, measure how often increased pressure interrupts charge. The answer determines whether the next change should add damage, movement, control or recovery.

Record the endgame state

  • Game version and save cleared through Epilogue 3.
  • Visible Challenge Mode options selected before leaving the house.
  • Armory loadout and current tooltips.
  • Chapter/encounter, DPS window, clear time and damage taken.
  • One changed variable for the next attempt.

This record turns a strong run into reusable player evidence. Without it, a high number cannot distinguish build power from an easier modifier, different encounter or lucky reward sequence.

Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock Challenge Mode in Garden of Witches?

Clear Epilogue 3, keep that cleared save and choose Continue.

Where do I enter Challenge Mode?

Continuing the Epilogue 3-cleared save places Sil in the Challenge Mode version of her house. Prepare the build, then leave through the door.

Why is Challenge Mode not showing?

Most often the wrong save was loaded or Epilogue 3 has not been cleared on that save. Verify both before changing the installation.
