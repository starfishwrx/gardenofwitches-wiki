Garden of Witches Map: Rooms, Rewards & Routes

Understand what changes between runs, read the reward before entering, and choose a route that keeps one working build alive.

Direct answer

Garden of Witches does not generate every room from scratch. The developer describes authored combat rooms whose placement and clear rewards are randomized. The map shows the reward type before entry, so choose by current health, loadout fit and risk instead of memorizing one path.

At every door

  1. Read the visible reward.
  2. Check whether health can support the risk.
  3. Ask which equipped action the reward improves.
  4. Keep one path toward recovery or a safer fight when available.
  5. Commit knowing the route moves forward.

Choose the next room with a four-question filter

1. Can health survive the next mistake?

When health is low enough that one ordinary error ends the chapter attempt, recovery is the build upgrade. A powerful Imprint has no value if Sil cannot reach the next major fight. Treat health as permission to take risk, not as a number to ignore until the boss.

2. Does the reward support an action you already land?

A beginner build becomes coherent when several choices improve the same repeatable action. For the default Scissors, that may mean creating a safer full-combo opening. For a charged weapon, it means enough space and direction to release. A reward tied to a missing trigger is not synergy; it is a promise the current loadout cannot keep.

3. Is the room teaching the same failure again?

If the prior rooms interrupted every combo, another pure damage reward does not solve the real issue. Choose control, movement, range or recovery that changes the failure. When a repeated authored room appears, use the same opening with one adjustment and compare the result.

4. What will you need before the major fight?

Do not spend the last safe movement option or replace a familiar skill immediately before a durable target. A route is successful when it reaches the fight with a readable loop, enough health and one exit action. Peak damage in an easy room is not the objective.

If the route choice went wrong

Do not rebuild every slot. Name the first failure: low health, interrupted input, missing target coverage or an unusable reward trigger. Change the next choice that addresses that failure. If the run ends, return home and preserve the lesson. The next map will differ, but the decision rule still works.

When a guide presents exact room probabilities or a universal route without a current capture, treat it as provisional. The useful fact is visible in your run: the reward shown, the health available and the action the build can actually repeat.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Garden of Witches map procedurally generated?

Not fully. The developer says combat rooms are authored, while room placement and clear rewards can vary between runs. Exact 1.0 reward labels should be read from the current map.

Can I go back to a previous room?

The developer's route explanation says you choose forward paths and cannot move backward or sideways, so inspect the visible reward before committing.

Which room reward should a beginner choose?

Choose recovery when health limits the run. Otherwise choose the reward that improves the attack, Spell or survival action you already use reliably.
