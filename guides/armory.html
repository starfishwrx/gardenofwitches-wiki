Garden of Witches Armory Guide: Unlocks & Loadouts

The Armory sets your baseline Scissors, Spells and Special Skill. Imprints and Relics reshape that baseline during a run.

Direct answer

In 1.0, the Armory is where you select unlocked Scissors, Spells and Special Skills. You begin with only the default Scissors and limited Spells; additional options unlock through chapter progression and other conditions.

Before entering a chapter

  1. Pick one Scissors whose input rhythm you can execute.
  2. Pick a Spell that covers the weapon’s weak moment.
  3. Give the Special Skill one job: engage, escape, setup or burst.
  4. Enter with one desired Imprint branch and one fallback.

What changes during the run

Imprints can change how a skill behaves. Relics connect separate skills. The Armory determines your starting loop, while drops determine how the loop branches.

Quick answer

Use the Armory before a run to equip unlocked Scissors, Spells and a Special Skill. Keep one primary damage rhythm, one answer to its unsafe moment and one fallback plan for random in-run rewards.

Missing unlock checklist

  1. Confirm the intended chapter or Epilogue was cleared on this save.
  2. Return to Sil's House instead of beginning another run immediately.
  3. Open the Armory and inspect every category.
  4. Read the current tooltip and compare the item against the equipped baseline.
  5. If absent, continue official story progress; do not rely on an unsourced exact unlock table.

A/B test a new unlock

Save the old loadout, equip one new component and enter the same progression state. Perform the new component's defining input correctly before comparing it. Record sustained DPS, clear time, interruptions and damage taken. If the new piece fixes the intended weakness without breaking the primary loop, keep it; otherwise restore the baseline and test a different slot.

Do not confuse an unlock with an upgrade. A newly available Scissors can be a different rhythm rather than a larger number. The Armory is a choice surface, not a straight power ladder.

Version boundary

Official 1.0 notes confirm Armory selection, limited starting options and additional unlocks through progress and other conditions. This guide does not invent a complete chapter-by-chapter unlock table.

Three Armory scenarios

New save: keep the default Scissors, learn its complete combo and choose a Spell that creates a safe opening. New unlock: change one slot and practice the defining input before comparing. Post-Epilogue 3: preserve the story-clear baseline for the first Challenge Mode test, then adapt from observed failure.

After every return, inspect permanent options before assuming a failed run produced nothing. When an expected item is absent, verify the correct save and progress state. Exact unlock conditions require direct evidence; this guide does not turn one player's timing into a universal rule.

Frequently asked questions

What can I equip in the Armory?

Once unlocked, you can freely choose your Scissors, Spells and Special Skill in the Armory.

Are all weapons unlocked at the start?

No. Version 1.0 starts with the default Scissors and a limited selection of Spells. More unlock through chapter progress and other conditions.
